Projector

Opens a window, filling this screen, that shows only the document in large type and follows the cursor. Click its title bar and press Windows key + Shift + → (or ←) to move it to the projected screen. Click Presenter view again to close it.

Tracked Changes

Works on the selected text, or on the whole document if nothing is selected, as soon as it is clicked. Work on a copy of the document! Votes must be recorded on all pending amendments before converting to Tracked Changes.
